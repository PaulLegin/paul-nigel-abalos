About

Technology with academic purpose and real-world impact.

I am an Assistant Professor in Information Technology at Pangasinan State University – San Carlos Campus, where I teach, conduct research, and develop technology solutions that bridge academia and industry. My work centers on artificial intelligence, data analytics, information systems, web development, and educational technology.

As both an educator and developer, I design and build web-based information systems, decision-support tools, and data-driven applications that improve organizational processes, support digital transformation, and enhance learning experiences.

I welcome opportunities for research collaboration, academic partnerships, technology projects, and professional networking.
