PickleDink: Real-Time Court Queue & Elo Rating Engine

Built for a club of competitive and professional pickleball players looking for a free, smooth queue and rating system without costly subscription fees.

Why I Built It

Most existing court management apps come with monthly subscription tiers and paid version locks. For our local club of competitive and professional pickleball players, physical whiteboard lists and paddle stacks were causing long arguments, while paid software wasn't practical. I built PickleDink to give our community a free, community-oriented automated kiosk that handles court rotations fairly.

Key Features & Architecture

Automated Rotation Algorithm

Balances match queueing across doubles and singles, ensuring players get equal court time and minimizing wait times between rounds.

Dynamic Elo Rating Engine

Calculates rating changes based on match results and instantly refreshes global club leaderboards after every game.

TV / Announcer Mode (Text-to-Speech)

A full-screen court display that utilizes the browser's Web Speech API to clearly announce called matches out loud over court noise.

The "Beeper" Silent Push Notification

Players check in with a quick QR scan. Their phones stay quiet until their court is called, vibrating and chiming only when it is their turn to play.

Identity Safeguards

Built-in duplicate name guards prevent accidental queue merges and require unique identifiers (like initials) so walk-in players never accidentally hijack someone else's spot.

Full-Stack Developer / Sole Creator & Architect

Tech Stack
  • Format: PWA / Single-Page Kiosk
  • Backend: PHP (REST endpoints)
  • Database: MySQL Normalized Schema
  • Frontend: Vanilla JavaScript, AJAX, Bootstrap
  • Hardware APIs: Web Speech API (TTS), jsQR
  • Access: Free for Community Use

SakayPH: Carpool & Chauffeur Service Platform

Built as a personal initiative to help carpool groups and drivers with secure escrow payments, document verification, and fair ride matching.

The Motivation & Story

Big ride-hailing services often take high percentage cuts from drivers, while informal carpool groups on social media often suffer from payment cancellations, no-shows, and lack of verified driver credentials. I took the personal initiative to build SakayPH as a working solution to help drivers and passengers connect securely. While the target driver community did not end up adopting it, I am proud of the engineering behind it — implementing robust concurrency controls, automated escrow release, and automated document verification.

Financial Integrity & Technical Highlights

Automated 24h Escrow Lock

Passenger payments are placed in escrow and released to the driver's wallet 24 hours after trip completion to protect both parties from disputes and fraud.

Concurrency Control via Row-Level Locking

Implemented transaction-safe payment workflows using MySQL/InnoDB transactions and row-level locking with SELECT ... FOR UPDATE to reduce race conditions during concurrent wallet and escrow operations.

Immutable Double-Entry Ledger

Every single transaction entering or leaving the platform is logged in an immutable ledger for auditability and reconciliation.

OCR Document Verification

Integrated with OCR.space API to scan and validate driver licenses and vehicle OR/CR documents during onboarding.

Security Architecture

Implements application-security practices including PDO prepared statements, anti-CSRF tokens on state-changing requests, and secure HTTP-only cookies for session handling.

La Mesa: Restaurant & Delivery Logistics System

A bespoke online ordering and delivery management system developed for my sister's restaurant business in Guernsey, Channel Islands (UK).

The Motivation & Background

Commercial delivery platforms take hefty commissions and don't natively support the unique geography of the Channel Islands (Guernsey's distinct parishes and GY1–GY8 postal codes). I built this platform specifically for my sister's restaurant in the UK to provide direct digital ordering, kitchen tracking, and delivery dispatch without relying exclusively on third-party marketplace platforms.

Features & Architecture

4-Sided Role Architecture

Separate portals for Super Admin, Store Owner (kitchen order pipeline), Delivery Rider (dispatch view at /rider), and Customers.

Guernsey Postcode Validation

Bidirectional sync that checks Guernsey GY postcodes against the island's 10 parishes to accurately calculate delivery coverage.

Real-Time Cart & Milestone Engine

Zero-reload AJAX cart updates with live progress meters for free delivery thresholds (£25) and instant delivery vs pickup fee calculations.

Automated E2E Testing Suite

Includes Playwright end-to-end test scripts and Axe-Core accessibility audits to verify cart arithmetic and checkout stability.

Payment Confirmation Hub

Includes 1-tap copy tools, Revolut Pay links, QR payments, and screenshot proof uploads with atomic order tracking numbers (FDC-YYYYMMDD-XXXX).

PSU Pageant Tabulation System

An institutional scoring and tabulation system co-engineered to replace error-prone paper scorecards with real-time digital criteria scoring and audit validation.

Problem & Context

Major university pageants and institutional competitions traditionally relied on physical scorecards collected by runners and manually transcribed into spreadsheets. Under live audience and stage pressure, manual score tallying caused prolonged delays, arithmetic discrepancies, and high audit stress. The objective was to engineer an automated, verifiable scoring system that delivers instantaneous tabulation with full audit transparency.

System Architecture & Engineering

Touch-Optimized Judge Kiosk

Responsive, single-purpose tablet interface enabling panel judges to review contestant details and submit category scores securely over local network.

Real-Time Central Audit Console

Administrative dashboard that monitors live judge submissions, verifies score completeness, and executes rank aggregation algorithms instantaneously.

Weighted Scoring & Tie-Breakers

Configurable criteria weighting formulas and automated tie-breaker logic to deliver consistent scoring, automated ranking, and transparent results.

Immutable Audit Log & Reports

Complete historical submission logs and instant generation of official signed tabulation summary sheets (PDF) for official board verification.

Intellectual Property & Governance

The system's original architecture, database normalization, and scoring algorithm are officially registered and protected under an IPOPHL Copyright Registration (Intellectual Property Office of the Philippines).
